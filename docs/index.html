ENGINEERING NOTES

The thinking behind WaveGauge.

Why we made specific low-level architectural decisions to ensure microsecond responsiveness.

ARCHITECTURE OCTOBER 2026

Why Electron and Web-Wrappers Were Strictly Rejected

Modern desktop utilities frequently ship bundled Chromium runtimes consuming 300MB+ RAM and 2-5% CPU just to display numbers. WaveGauge was built in native C# .NET WPF, compiling to an ultra-lean binary that uses ~30MB RAM and virtually 0% CPU.

Agilitiq Ltd Core Systems
WIN32 INTERNALS OCTOBER 2026

Deep Dive into WS_EX_NOACTIVATE and Focus Guarding

If you click a taskbar overlay while playing a competitive game, a standard window steals keyboard focus and minimizes your game. WaveGauge injects WS_EX_NOACTIVATE and WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW into the Win32 window styles, preventing focus theft completely.

Agilitiq Ltd Core Systems
PACKAGING OCTOBER 2026

True Single-File Native Library Self-Extraction

WPF applications typically require loose DirectX and presentation DLLs. By utilizing MSBuild single-file bundling with IncludeNativeLibrariesForSelfExtract and single-file compression, WaveGauge distributes as a 100% standalone executable.
